Sorted from calm (samatha) to insight (vipassanā). The marker on each tile shows which one leads, or whether the practice works on both together.

The calm-to-insight positions and some tradition tags are simplified judgment calls, not official doctrine. Many practices are shared more widely than a few tags can show, and every practice develops both calm and insight to some degree.
