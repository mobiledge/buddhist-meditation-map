Buddhist Traditions & Schools

The living branches of Buddhism with their regional traditions, schools, monastic orders and teaching lineages. Click any entry to read about it and to see the meditation practices associated with it.

Each entry is labelled with what it is, because the levels are not alike: Theravāda divides into monastic orders that share one doctrine, while Mahāyāna divides into schools with different teachings.

    This tree is simplified. It leaves out extinct schools and many smaller lineages, and dates for early figures are traditional. Practice links show where a practice is most characteristic, and many are shared more widely.
