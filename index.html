Buddhist Traditions & Schools

The living branches of Buddhism, read from left to right: branches, then regional traditions, schools, monastic orders and teaching lineages. Each entry links to its Wikipedia article and to its meditation practices.

Each entry is labelled with what it is, because the levels are not alike: Theravāda divides into monastic orders that share one doctrine, while Mahāyāna divides into schools with different teachings.

Use the + on a node to open the level below it.

This tree is simplified. It leaves out extinct schools and many smaller lineages. Vajrayāna is placed inside Mahāyāna, whose goal and philosophy it shares, though it is often listed as a third branch. Practice links show where a practice is most characteristic, and many are shared more widely.
